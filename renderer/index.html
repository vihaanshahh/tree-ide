Tree IDE

An IDE that thinks in modules, not files. See your codebase as labeled cards, audit any one of them, or swap one out for something else with one click.

  • Open any repo. Each top-level directory becomes a card on the map.
  • Click a card. Right panel shows the files inside and three actions: Audit, Swap with…, Explain.
  • Swap describes the replacement, AI plans it, you preview the diff, you click apply.

Uses your Claude subscription via the claude CLI.
